The Adobe years: six chapters

Aug 2019 to Sep 2025, as Senior Business & Visualization Analyst. Each chapter is a problem I walked into and what changed afterwards. XP unlocks as you scroll.

  1. Chapter 1: The client whisperer

    +100 XP

    I managed high-value B2B campaign accounts and was the person clients called first. Part of the job was listening for what they hadn't said yet, then getting the right people in our building to act on it.

    Client retention up 20%, on-time delivery with 98% client satisfaction, expansion work that grew revenue 15%.listen first, chart later

  2. Chapter 2: The plumber

    +100 XP

    Reports were slow because the pipes underneath were messy. I rolled out Power BI, Tableau and MSBI across departments, reworked the data models and ETL, built dashboards on Databricks pipelines and automated the financial reporting feeds.

    Data processing time down 20%, operational efficiency up 15%, manual data prep cut by 30%.nobody thanks the pipes, but everyone notices when they leak

  3. Chapter 3: The detective

    +100 XP

    When funnel numbers didn't make sense, I ran exploratory data analysis and root cause analysis on the conversion data, reworked the attribution model and cleaned the data in Databricks. I also built Salesforce CRM reports that pulled Adobe Analytics, Workfront and Tableau data together for forecasting.

    Funnel accuracy and efficiency up 18%, contributing to a 10% increase in campaign ROI.ask "why" until it gets awkward

  4. Chapter 4: The stage

    +100 XP

    Data only matters if people read it. I ran the analytics for Adobe Summit ROI and conversion, built a self-serve Media Dashboard so leaders could check cross-channel performance on their own, and designed dashboards and SSRS reports people actually opened.

    Reporting adoption and accuracy up 30% across teams.the best dashboard is the one that gets opened

  5. Chapter 5: The forecaster

    +100 XP

    I built financial models in Excel, Power BI and Tableau on Databricks data: revenue forecasts, investment scenarios and sensitivity analysis, so budget conversations started from numbers instead of opinions.

    Financial planning accuracy up 20%, and it informed budget allocation.what if we're wrong? run it again

  6. Chapter 6: The team player

    +100 XP

    I took requirements from marketing, business and analytics stakeholders across APAC, EMEA and the US, and ran Scrum ceremonies in Jira (sprint planning, backlog grooming) so BI enhancements stayed on schedule and visible. Within six months of joining, I won the Star Spot Award.

    Three regions, one backlog, one award.sticky notes were involved
